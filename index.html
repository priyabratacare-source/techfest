GOT QUESTIONS?

FREQUENTLY ASKED.

Everything you need to know about TTK TechFest 2026.

What is TTK TechFest 2026?

+

TTK TechFest is the flagship annual technical festival of TTK College of Engineering. It brings together technical competitions, hands-on workshops, exhibitions, and guest lectures designed to spotlight student talent and innovation.

Who can participate in TTK TechFest?

+

The fest is open to students from colleges across India. Depending on the specific event, participants can take part either solo or as part of a team.

How can I register for the events?

+

Registration happens through the official TTK TechFest website. Each event has its own registration link along with detailed rules and guidelines you'll need to follow.

Are there any participation or registration fees?

+

Entry to most events is free. That said, a few specialized workshops or competitions may carry a small registration fee to cover material or resource costs.

Will participants get certificates or prizes?

+

Absolutely — every participant receives a certificate, while winners take home prizes, certificates of excellence, and additional goodies.
